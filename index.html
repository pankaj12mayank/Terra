FAQ

Questions, answered.

Do I need an account?
No. Each visit gets a scoped session with its own queue, settings and storage. Your session ID is shown in the workspace so you can come back to it.
Where do the images come from?
From configured source providers (Pexels, Pixabay and custom endpoints). If one provider fails or is rate-limited, the job automatically falls back to the next one.
What happens to failed items?
Failures stay in the review grid. You can retry them, swap them to another provider, or remove them — the rest of the batch is unaffected.
How big can a batch be?
Row limits, CSV size and daily job caps are set by the operator and enforced server-side. The workspace shows the limit before you start.
Can I change the theme?
Yes — light and dark, toggled from any screen and remembered for your next visit.

Ready to run your first batch?

Open the workspace, load a few images and watch the pipeline go. No setup, no card, no waiting.

FAQ

Why did an item fail?
Usually a dead URL or a rate-limited provider. Retry it, or swap it to another provider from the Review step.
Can I change settings mid-run?
Save them — the next job picks them up. The running job keeps the settings it started with.
What does “Skipped” mean?
The item already existed in the batch and duplicate skipping is on, so it was left untouched.
How do I stop a job?
Hit Stop in the sticky bar. Finished items stay; unfinished ones remain queued as failed/stopped for a retry.
